Self-Motivated · Independent · Persistent Researcher

I am interested in understanding how molecular structure, dynamics, and interfaces govern the behavior of energy and molecular materials.

My experimental research focuses on oxide thin-film electrodes, polymer electrolytes, and full-cell electrochemical systems, combining materials synthesis, electrochemical testing, and operando characterization such as Raman spectroscopy and SAXS/WAXS to study structural evolution under working conditions.

Building on this experimental background, I am expanding into machine-learning-driven molecular simulation using MACE-based molecular dynamics and DFT references. My long-term goal is to connect experimentally observed behavior with molecular-level mechanisms and develop multiscale approaches for complex molecular materials.

Research

My research explores how processing, structure, molecular dynamics, and interfaces govern the behavior of electrochemical materials. I have progressively expanded from experimental materials synthesis and characterization to operando analysis and atomistic simulation, with the long-term goal of connecting experimentally observed phenomena with machine-learning-driven molecular and multiscale models.

Process–Structure–Property Relationships in Thin-Film Electrodes

WO3 · Spray pyrolysis · Raman · XRD · SEM · Electrochemistry

I investigated how fabrication conditions influence the structure and electrochemical behavior of WO3 thin-film electrodes. By combining controlled synthesis with structural, morphological, and electrochemical characterization, I examined how processing parameters translate into changes in material structure and charge-storage behavior.

Operando Structural Evolution of Polymer Electrolytes

PVDF-HFP · Polymer electrolytes · Raman · SAXS/WAXS · Full-cell electrochemistry

My current experimental research focuses on PVDF-HFP-based polymer electrolytes and their structural evolution during battery operation. I combine polymer synthesis, electrochemical testing, in-situ Raman spectroscopy, and operando SAXS/WAXS to investigate how molecular and mesoscale structural changes relate to electrochemical performance.

Machine-Learning-Driven Atomistic Simulation

MACE · Molecular dynamics · External electric fields · DFT · Molecular response

To move beyond phenomenological interpretation of experimental observations, I am developing atomistic simulation approaches using machine-learning interatomic potentials. My current work uses MACE-based molecular dynamics under external electric fields, together with DFT references, to study molecular response and connect microscopic dynamics with experimentally observable behavior.

FUTURE DIRECTIONS

Toward Multiscale Molecular Materials Modeling

ML potentials · Organic molecular crystals · ML-based coarse graining · Multiscale modeling

I aim to develop machine-learning-driven simulation frameworks that bridge atomistic and mesoscale descriptions of molecular materials, enabling access to longer length and time scales while connecting molecular mechanisms with experimentally observable behavior.
