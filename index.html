Yeeun Lee

Materials Science & Engineering

I am interested in understanding how structure, molecular dynamics, and interfaces govern the performance of energy materials.

My current research focuses on PVDF-HFP polymer electrolytes, operando characterization, and electrochemical behavior. I use techniques including Raman spectroscopy and SAXS/WAXS to connect structural evolution with battery performance.

More recently, I have been expanding from experimental materials research toward machine-learning-driven molecular simulation, with the goal of connecting experimentally observed behavior to molecular-scale mechanisms and, ultimately, multiscale models.
