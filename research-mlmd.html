Machine-Learning Molecular Dynamics of Electric-Field-Induced Vibrational Dynamics

This study investigates molecular response and vibrational dynamics under external electric fields using machine-learning molecular dynamics. Pretrained MACE machine-learning interatomic potentials were used to generate molecular-dynamics trajectories under multiple electric-field conditions, enabling atomistic tracking of field-induced structural and dynamical responses over time.

The resulting trajectories were analyzed using Python to quantify atomic and molecular motion, time-dependent dipole response, infrared spectra, power spectra, and mode-resolved vibrational energy. Particular attention was given to field-dependent vibrational excitation, anharmonic coupling, and energy redistribution between molecular degrees of freedom.

Machine-learning molecular-dynamics results were compared with density-functional-theory reference calculations and experimentally relevant spectral behavior to evaluate the physical fidelity and applicable range of the MACE potential. This combined workflow provides an atomistic framework for connecting molecular motion, vibrational response, and externally driven material behavior.
