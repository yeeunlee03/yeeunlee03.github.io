Machine-Learning Molecular Dynamics of Electric-Field-Induced Vibrational Dynamics

This study uses machine-learning molecular dynamics to investigate molecular motion and vibrational response under external electric fields. Molecular-dynamics trajectories were generated under different electric-field conditions using pretrained MACE machine-learning interatomic potentials in a Linux and Slurm-based HPC environment. The trajectories were analyzed using Python to track time-dependent atomic and molecular motion and dipole response, as well as changes in vibrational energy for individual normal modes.

External electric fields altered the vibrational response of molecular bonds, and these field-dependent changes were analyzed through IR spectra calculated from the simulated dipole time series. Power spectra and normal-mode-resolved vibrational energies were further examined to investigate vibrational behavior, anharmonic coupling, and energy-transfer pathways. The machine-learning molecular-dynamics results were compared with DFT reference calculations and experimental results to evaluate the physical trends reproduced by the model and its range of applicability.
