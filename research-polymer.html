Co-Solvent Processing and Operando Structure of PVDF-HFP/Succinonitrile Polymer Electrolytes

This project studies how the casting co-solvent controls the structure and lithium-ion transport of PVDF-HFP/succinonitrile (SN)/LiTFSI quasi-solid electrolytes. Membranes are solution-cast from DMF (D10) or from DMF:THF mixtures (7:3, D7T3; 5:5, D5T5) and dried under a controlled, stepwise schedule (Ar glovebox, then stepped vacuum at ≤ 40 °C) so that the plasticizing SN stays in the film.

The co-solvent visibly changes the film: DMF-cast membranes are opaque white, whereas DMF:THF-cast membranes are translucent, pointing to differences in porosity and crystallinity. The conductivity follows Arrhenius behaviour between 30 and 70 °C, with the lowest activation energy for DMF:THF-cast D5T5 (0.27 eV). Oxidation starts at about 4.35–4.38 V vs Li+/Li, and Li|Li cells cycle with nearly identical overpotentials over 146 h. Ongoing work combines ex situ SAXS/WAXS, SEM and TGA with operando SAXS/WAXS in window coin cells to follow how the membrane nanostructure responds to lithium plating and stripping.
